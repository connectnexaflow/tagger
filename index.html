▶ How to Play
🏷️ One player is randomly chosen as THE TAGGER
🏃 The tagger must touch another player within 30 seconds
🔁 On touch → tagged player becomes the new tagger, timer resets
💀 Time runs out → current tagger is eliminated
🏆 Last player standing wins!
